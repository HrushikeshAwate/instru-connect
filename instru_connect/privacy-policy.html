InstruConnect — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

InstruConnect ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is an official application for the Instrumentation Department. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide the App's features:

  • Account information: your official college email address and display name, obtained when you sign in with your college Microsoft account.
  • Profile information: your name, MIS/enrollment number, department, batch and academic year, contact number, and parent/guardian contact number.
  • Academic activity data: attendance records, events, achievements, certifications, complaints/grievances, notices, and resources associated with your account.
  • Device & notification data: a push-notification token used to deliver notifications to your device.
  • Diagnostics & crash data: anonymized crash reports and diagnostic information (via Firebase Crashlytics) to help us fix problems and improve stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and provide access to the App using your official college account.
  • To display and manage your academic information such as timetables, attendance, events, certifications, achievements, and resources.
  • To send you relevant notifications (for example, about events, notices, or attendance).
  • To respond to complaints or grievances you submit within the App.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the reliability and performance of the App.

3. How Your Information Is Stored

Your information is stored and processed using Google Firebase services (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Storage, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Cloud Functions, and Firebase Crashlytics). Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We rely on Google's infrastructure security to help protect your data. Please review the Firebase Privacy and Security information for more details.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties for advertising. Information may be visible to authorized department staff and administrators for legitimate academic and administrative purposes (for example, viewing attendance or responding to a complaint). Service providers such as Google Firebase process data on our behalf solely to operate the App.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App's services and to comply with the department's academic and administrative requirements. Certain records (such as resolved complaints) are retained for a limited period and then removed automatically.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.
  • You may disable push notifications from your device settings at any time.
  • To request account or data deletion, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by students, faculty, and staff of the Instrumentation Department. It is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
